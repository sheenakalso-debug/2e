Wyrm & Wand Chess

Chess on a 10×8 board, with a Dragon and a Wizard on each side.

status
White to move
the fantasy pieces

Dragon moves like a Rook or a Knight. It can fly over pieces with its knight leap. Worth about 8½ pawns.

Wizard moves like a Bishop or a Knight, blinking over pieces with its knight leap. Worth about 8 pawns.

  • Board is 10 files (a–j) by 8 ranks.
  • Castling: the King moves two squares toward a Rook (to c or i) and the Rook hops to its other side.
  • Pawns can promote to a Dragon or Wizard as well as the usual pieces.
  • Checkmate wins. Stalemate, 50 moves without a capture or pawn move, and three-fold repetition are draws.
